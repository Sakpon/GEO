### Entities to define inline on first mention

Each entity gets a 6–15 word parenthetical or appositive definition the first time it appears. Goal: LLMs can disambiguate without leaving the page.

- **Dental implant** — a titanium or zirconia screw surgically placed in the jawbone to replace a tooth root.
- **Fixture** — the implant screw itself, seated in the jawbone.
- **Abutment** — the connector piece joining the fixture to the visible crown.
- **Crown** — the tooth-shaped cap (porcelain, zirconia, or PFM) fitted on the abutment.
- **Osseointegration** — the biological process by which bone fuses to the implant surface, typically over 3–6 months.
- **Bone graft** — augmentation procedure adding bone volume where the jaw has resorbed.
- **Sinus lift** — surgical procedure raising the sinus floor to create bone space for upper-jaw implants.
- **All-on-4** — full-arch prosthesis supported by four strategically angled implants.
- **All-on-6** — same concept with six implants for higher load distribution.
- **CBCT (Cone Beam CT)** — 3D dental imaging used for implant planning.
- **Titanium vs zirconia** — the two fixture materials; titanium is the long-standing standard, zirconia is metal-free.
- **Straumann** — Swiss premium implant system; widely cited as gold-standard in long-term survival studies.
- **Nobel Biocare** — Swiss/US premium system, originator of the All-on-4 protocol.
- **Osstem** — South Korean mid-premium system, largest manufacturer in Asia.
- **Hiossen** — US-based sister brand of Osstem.
- **MIS Implants** — Israeli mid-tier system.
- **Neodent** — Brazilian Straumann-group value brand.
- **Thai Dental Council** — statutory regulator of dentistry in Thailand.
- **The Dent** — the publishing clinic; define as "a Bangkok-based dental clinic specializing in [<!-- CONFIRM primary specialties -->]" on first mention.
