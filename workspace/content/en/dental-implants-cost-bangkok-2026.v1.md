## What's included in the implant price — and what isn't?

A typical Bangkok implant package covers the consultation, imaging, surgery, components, final crown, and a defined follow-up window. What it does *not* cover are surgical add-ons triggered by your anatomy, such as bone grafting or sinus lifts. Knowing both lists before you fly prevents quote shock on arrival.

**Usually included**

- Initial consultation and treatment planning
- CBCT (Cone Beam CT) 3D scan for implant planning
- Digital impressions or conventional moulds
- Surgical placement of the fixture
- The abutment component
- The final crown (porcelain, zirconia, or PFM)
- Standard post-operative follow-ups
- Manufacturer warranty on the implant fixture

**Usually excluded**

- Tooth extraction of the failing tooth
- Bone graft or sinus lift
- IV sedation or general anaesthesia
- Temporary restorations between visits
- Night guard after final placement

## Are cheap dental implants in Thailand safe? How do I judge clinic quality?

Safety in Thailand is not about price — it is about the clinician, the brand, and the protocol. All practising dentists in Thailand must be registered with the Thai Dental Council, and reputable Bangkok clinics use the same FDA- and CE-marked implant systems as their Western counterparts. The risk lies with outlier operators, not with the country.

## How long will I need to stay in Bangkok for implant treatment?

Most medical-tourism implant cases use a two-visit protocol split by a healing window for osseointegration — the biological process by which bone fuses to the implant surface, typically over 3–6 months. <!-- SOURCE NEEDED: ADA patient resource or peer-reviewed JOMI meta-analysis. --> Expect 5–7 days on the first trip and 5–7 days on the second; same-day "immediate load" cases are possible in selected anatomies.

## Myth vs fact

**Myth: Cheaper implants must mean lower quality.**
Fact: Bangkok clinics routinely use the same Swiss and Korean implant systems sold in London, Sydney, and New York. Price reflects local overhead, not hardware.

**Myth: Thai dentists aren't as qualified as Western dentists.**
Fact: Implant specialists in Thailand complete the same undergraduate dental degree plus post-graduate implantology programmes, and must be registered with the Thai Dental Council to practise.

**Myth: You have to stay in Thailand for months.**
Fact: The osseointegration window is spent at home. Most patients fly in for two short trips of 5–7 days each, roughly 3–6 months apart.

**Myth: The online price is always the final price.**
Fact: The online price covers a standard case. Bone grafts, sinus lifts, extractions, and sedation are priced separately, which is why a written, CBCT-based treatment plan is essential before you travel.

## FAQ

### How much does a single dental implant cost in Bangkok in 2026?

A complete single implant — fixture, abutment, and crown — typically costs <!-- CONFIRM: THB 45,000–90,000 --> (USD 1,250–2,500) in Bangkok in 2026. The final number depends on the implant brand, the crown material, and whether any add-on procedures such as bone grafting are required.

### Why are dental implants so much cheaper in Thailand?

Lower clinic overheads, in-country dental labs, competitive pricing among Bangkok specialists, and favourable exchange rates together reduce the cost by roughly 50–70% compared with the UK, US, or Australia — without changing the implant brand or surgical protocol used.

### Are dental implants in Thailand safe and regulated?

Yes. Every practising dentist in Thailand must register with the Thai Dental Council, and reputable Bangkok clinics use FDA- and CE-marked implant systems with full documentation. Safety depends on choosing a clinic that uses a named brand, CBCT planning, and issues a written warranty.

### What implant brands does a Bangkok clinic typically offer?

Established Bangkok clinics typically offer a choice between premium Swiss systems (Straumann, Nobel Biocare) and mid-premium Korean systems (Osstem, Hiossen). Some also stock Neodent or MIS. Ask which brand is included in your quote and request the manufacturer's reference number.

### Does the implant package price include the crown and abutment?

At reputable Bangkok clinics, the quoted "implant package" price includes the fixture, abutment, and final crown. Bone grafting, sinus lifts, extractions, sedation, and temporary restorations are priced separately because they depend on individual anatomy.

### How long does the full implant process take if I'm flying in from abroad?

Plan for two trips of 5–7 days, separated by 3–6 months of osseointegration at home. The first trip covers surgery; the second covers the final crown. Same-day "immediate load" protocols are possible in selected cases after CBCT assessment.

### How much does All-on-4 cost in Bangkok compared to the UK or Australia?

All-on-4 in Bangkok costs around <!-- CONFIRM: THB 380,000–650,000 --> per arch (USD 10,500–18,000), compared with GBP 12,000–25,000 in the UK and AUD 25,000–35,000 in Australia — a saving of roughly 50–70% on the same procedure. <!-- SOURCE NEEDED -->

### What should I check before booking a Bangkok implant clinic?

Confirm Thai Dental Council registration, the exact implant brand and reference number, whether CBCT planning is standard, what the package includes and excludes, the written warranty terms, and the clinic's remote follow-up protocol for international patients.
