# Brand Voice — thedent.co.th

> Seed guidelines. Refine with the client's approved style guide when available.

## Positioning

Modern, trustworthy, clinically competent, warm. Not "discount dentist." Not "luxury spa." Calm expertise that reassures anxious patients and satisfies informed ones.

## Voice attributes

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Plain, clear explanations of procedures | Jargon without a one-line translation |
| Reassuring, second-person ("you'll feel…") | Cold clinical or sales-y copy |
| Evidence-based language ("studies show", "per ADA guidelines") | Medical claims without a source |
| Specific numbers (duration, recovery days) | Vague superlatives ("the best", "world-class") that can't be substantiated |
| Acknowledge patient fear, then defuse it | Dismiss concerns or minimize pain |

## Reading level

- **Thai:** Everyday Thai, avoid overly formal royal register unless context demands it. Clinical terms explained in parentheses.
- **English:** Grade 8–9 readability. Short sentences. One idea per paragraph.

## Bilingual rules

- Each URL has one primary language. Do not mix languages within body copy.
- Always use `hreflang` pairs for TH ↔ EN equivalents.
- Do not machine-translate English → Thai and publish. Always have a Thai writer adapt, not translate.
- Proper nouns (doctor names, brand names like Invisalign, Straumann) stay in original script.
- Prices in THB. For EN pages aimed at medical tourists, show approximate USD/EUR in parentheses where appropriate.

## Formatting conventions

- **Headings:** Question-shaped H2s where possible ("How long do dental implants last?"). Great for SEO and GEO both.
- **Lists:** Use bullets for parallel items, numbered lists only for true sequences (procedure steps).
- **Tables:** Use for comparisons (e.g. Invisalign vs. braces). LLMs extract tables cleanly.
- **First line of every article:** A direct, complete-sentence answer to the article's question. (This is the GEO-citable snippet.)
- **CTAs:** Specific action + low-friction verb ("Book a free consultation", not "Learn more").

## Compliance guardrails

- Never guarantee medical outcomes.
- Before/after photos require patient consent and must comply with Thai Medical Council rules.
- Price ranges acceptable; exact prices should link to a "pricing" page that the clinic updates.
- Avoid comparative claims against named competitors.

## Trust signals to include when relevant

- Doctor's name + credentials byline
- Last-reviewed date (medical content)
- Citations to reputable sources (ADA, WHO, peer-reviewed journals)
- Clinic certifications
