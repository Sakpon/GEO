# Optimization Goals — thedent.co.th

## Primary objectives

1. **Rank** for high-intent dental queries in Bangkok (Thai + English).
2. **Get cited** by generative answer engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, Claude) for dental questions relevant to our services.
3. **Convert** informed visitors into consultation bookings.

## North-star KPIs

| Metric | Source | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| Organic sessions (TH + EN split) | GA4 | Weekly |
| Consultation-booking conversions | GA4 event / CRM | Weekly |
| Non-branded keyword rankings (top 10) | Search Console / rank tracker | Weekly |
| Share of voice vs. 5 named competitors | Rank tracker | Monthly |
| AI-citation count (ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews) | Manual + monitoring tool | Monthly |
| Branded search volume | Search Console / Google Trends | Monthly |

## Priority service clusters (tentative — confirm with client)

Rank order drives editorial weighting. Adjust after client confirms revenue priority.

1. **Dental implants** — high AOV, high medical-tourism interest
2. **Invisalign / clear aligners** — high AOV, high local + expat interest
3. **Veneers / cosmetic** — high AOV, high tourism interest
4. **General dentistry** — volume driver, feeds recall revenue
5. **Orthodontics (braces)** — long engagement cycle

## Target geos

- **Primary:** Bangkok Metropolitan Area (Thai locals + resident expats)
- **Secondary:** Thailand nationwide (English-speaking visitors)
- **Tertiary (medical tourism source countries):** UK, Australia, USA, Germany, Nordics, Gulf states, China

Local SEO: one Google Business Profile per branch, consistent NAP, bilingual posts.

## SEO targets (first 6 months)

- Complete technical audit with zero P0/P1 issues open
- Top-10 ranking for at least 10 high-intent non-branded keywords per priority cluster
- Schema markup on all service, location, and FAQ pages
- Internal-link graph with no orphan service pages
- Core Web Vitals: all "Good"

## GEO targets (first 6 months)

- Publish "llms.txt" and verify discoverability
- One citation-optimized long-form article per priority cluster (question-dense, entity-clear, schema-rich)
- Appear as a cited source in Perplexity for at least 5 target questions per cluster
- Appear in Google AI Overviews for at least 3 high-intent queries
- Track mentions in ChatGPT (with web browsing) for brand + service queries
